## 🚀 In Progress (Q1 2025)

### Daily Ritual System
- 🔄 **"Breathe, Move, Protect" Morning Ritual** (7 minutes)
  - 2-minute guided breathing (adaptive to air quality)
  - 3-minute light movement/stretches
  - 2-minute mindful environmental scan
  - Streak tracking & badges
  - Daily completion rewards

### Micro-Coaching Intelligence
- 🔄 **Smart Environmental Prompts**
  - Humidity-based breathing guidance
  - AQI-triggered protection tips
  - CO₂ ventilation reminders
  - Temperature-adaptive advice
  - Weather-based activity suggestions

### Wellness Journal
- 🔄 **Simple Daily Logging**
  - One-line mood check ("better/same/worse")
  - Emoji + optional notes
  - Correlation insights (e.g., "When PM2.5 was lower, you felt better 4/5 days")
  - Weekly wellness summaries

### Environmental Recovery Tips
- 🔄 **Daily Wellness Recipes**
  - Smoggy day → Warm ginger tea
  - Dry air → Steam inhalation ritual
  - High pollen → Nasal rinse routine
  - Personalized based on conditions
