# 🌊 Water Quality Quest

## Environmental Adventure | Strategy | Puzzle-Quest

### Overview
Water Quality Quest is an educational game with 30 progressive chapters teaching environmental science, water quality monitoring, and community engagement through interactive gameplay.

### Game Features
- **30 Chapters** with increasing difficulty (1-10 stars)
- **Progressive Learning** from basic water testing to global policy
- **Interactive Minigames** including sampling, investigation, crisis management
- **Real-world Science** based on actual water quality indicators
- **Decision-Making** that affects trust levels and outcomes
- **Achievement System** with badges and rewards

### How to Play
1. Open `water-quality-quest.html` in a web browser
2. Complete each chapter's challenge
3. Earn Eco Points and build Trust
4. Unlock new equipment and abilities
5. Progress through all 30 chapters to become a Master Eco Ranger

### Educational Goals
- Water quality indicators (pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen)
- Environmental data collection and analysis
- Pollution sources and transport
- Community engagement and science communication
- Systems thinking and trade-offs
- Global environmental policy

### Game Mechanics
- **Trust Level**: Affected by dialogue choices and community engagement
- **Eco Points**: Earned by completing challenges successfully
- **Water Quality**: Improves as you solve pollution problems
- **Data Collected**: Tracks your scientific progress
- **Inventory**: Unlocked tools and equipment

### Chapter Progression
- **Chapters 1-10**: Observation & Data Collection
- **Chapters 11-20**: Analysis & Problem Solving
- **Chapters 21-25**: Action & Implementation
- **Chapters 26-30**: Leadership & Global Impact

### Installation
Simply place all three files in the same directory:
- `water-quality-quest.html`
- `water-quest-styles.css`
- `water-quest-game.js`

No additional dependencies required!
